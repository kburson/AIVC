### R1-F001 — Accepted and addressed

Confirmed from the plan's transition ordering that canceling source handles does not itself resume an AudioContext, and no outstanding audio could trigger the old stall watchdog. Tasks 6 and 8 now distinguish canceled audio from the reusable context's state.

Playback gains serialized `ensureRunning(token)` and `resetAfterCancel()` contracts. Confirmation stops/clears old sources first, then waits for any pending suspension and resumes the empty context. Recovery respects a newer tentative candidate's pause; mic-mute/overlong-discard restoration is allowed only for a still-current answer. A canceled generation is never replayed.

After watchdog teardown, the failed answer stays speech-disabled, but a later generation can recover the context. Before first synthesis admission the queue actively ensures a running context; rejected or still-suspended recovery beyond two wall-clock seconds becomes visible SPEECH_RESUME_FAILED even when zero audio is outstanding, while text/history remain usable. This removes the silent wait state without issuing paid retries.

Required tests cover suspend resolving both before and after confirmation, Speaking and Working/no-audio entry, the complete end/new-answer scheduling path, watchdog recovery, newer-candidate replacement, rejected/hung resume, and browser Beacon audio scheduling after voice interruption.
